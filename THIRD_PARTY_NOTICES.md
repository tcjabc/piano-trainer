## Vadivam icons

Source: https://github.com/praveenjuge/vadivam

Piano Trainer uses Vadivam icons in two ways:

- **Unmodified icons**, inlined at build time from the `@iconify-json/vadivam` package (the names are listed in `src/ui/icon-list.ts`).
- **`src/assets/hand-right.svg`**, a modified version of Vadivam's "hand" icon. A thumb was added in the same stroke style, along with five fingertip circles that the app fills with its finger colours. The app draws the left hand by mirroring this file.
